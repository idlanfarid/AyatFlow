# AyatFlow
AyatFlow (مصحف الإمام) is an intelligent, hands-free Quran teleprompter for Imams, huffaz, and reciters during prayer and Tarawih. Using real-time Arabic voice tracking, it follows your recitation, scrolls verse by verse, and auto-flips pages as you read—letting you focus on Khushu' and Tajweed without ever touching your screen.
